drillpad is an independent study project: a course, practice problems and a running engineering journal for software-engineering interview preparation.

## What is verified and what is not

Most lessons were drafted by AI models from my prompts and outlines and have not had a systematic accuracy review. A lesson carries a `Last verified: YYYY-MM-DD` line only after I have checked it against primary sources. Treat everything else as study notes, not sources. Claims I have not confirmed are tagged `[VERIFY]`.
