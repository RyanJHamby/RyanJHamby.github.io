Swolverine Support

Need help or want to report a problem? Open an issue at github.com/RyanJHamby/RyanJHamby.github.io/issues (please don't include personal or health information in a public issue).

Common questions

How do I delete my account? Settings → Account → Delete account and data.

How do I restore my data on a new phone? Install Swolverine and sign in with the same account. Your training history is restored automatically.

I didn't get the verification email. Check spam, then tap “Resend email” on the verification screen.
