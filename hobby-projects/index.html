App Store

M-HEAL Solar Fridge#

Project Team Lead & Electrical Sub Team Lead | Jan 2019 - Dec 2022

Solar Fridge is a student-run project team at Michigan Health Engineered for All Lives. Our 15-member team iterates through insulation and thermoelectric refrigeration designs to cool vaccine coolers.

Key Achievements:

  • Improved the amount of time that dorm fridges can sustain a 2-8°C range by over 800%
  • Created a touchscreen LCD digital data logger that displays current and previous vaccine temperatures
  • Designed PCB to condense thermocouple leads and implemented Arduino code driver

May 2020 - Sept 2021

  • Backed user data using Google Firebase for persistent storage
